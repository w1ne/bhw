# BHW Conversations publishing template

Keep it simple: a useful founder story, readable faces, a full interview, and one clear invitation.

## Article

- Title: [Guest name]: [specific, human story or idea]
- Metadata: [Interviewer] with [Guest] · [Place] · Recorded [verified date]
- Embed: the full public interview on @BudapestHardwareClub. State the actual duration and language.
- Opening: [everyday problem or surprising detail]. [Guest], founder of [linked project], joined us in [place].
- Story: a personal detail, the origin of the idea, the practical problem, the turning point, and what comes next. Aim for 300–450 words.
- Images: two relevant project images beside the paragraphs they explain. Include descriptive alt text, intrinsic dimensions, and linked source captions. Label concepts as concepts; do not present renders or estimates as tested hardware.
- Closing: [specific help the guest needs], then links to the project, guest profile, full video, and BHW community.
- Follow the site's English/Hungarian pattern. Verify names, quotes, dates, and translations against the recording and research.

## Video and imagery

- Preserve the agreed full conversation and original speech. Use restrained exposure correction, clear audio, and a few explanatory cutaways with short transitions.
- Homepage photo: real interview moment with both faces readable at phone size. Retain the original, version replacements, and inspect the final result.
- Thumbnail: real guest, simple BHW branding, short readable topic hook; avoid misleading expressions or promises.
- Title: [specific topic/question] | [Guest], [Project]. Put the topic first.
- Description: one-sentence premise, guest and project links, article link, recording context, and chapters beginning at 00:00.
- Review captions by listening, especially names and technical terms, before uploading them. Automatic captions do not replace a reviewed track.
- Add the series playlist and a relevant subscribe/end-screen element when available; verify these separately from the rendered closing card.

## Short LinkedIn company-page post

[One concrete opening line about the problem.]

For our latest BHW Conversation, we met [Guest], founder of [Project], in [Place]. We talked about [human detail], [practical idea], and [one useful lesson].

[Guest]'s next step: [specific, verified request or milestone].

Read the story and watch the full [duration] interview:
[article URL]

[One relevant question inviting a useful response.]

#Hardware #Founders #BudapestHardwareClub

Publish as Budapest Hardware Club, using the article preview or a clear interview photo. Verify the selected company identity before posting and save the published permalink afterward. Mention people only when their identities are verified. Keep the post short and avoid claims about guaranteed reach or algorithm tricks.

## Publication checks

- Site contract and build pass; exact main commit deployment succeeds.
- Read back public article, homepage asset, share image, video ID/duration, and source links.
- Check desktop and phone layout, image loading, language switching, and signed-out video playback.
- Confirm YouTube channel identity, public visibility, custom thumbnail, chapters, and available HD resolution.
- Confirm the LinkedIn post appears under the BHW company Page and save its permalink.
- After enough impressions accumulate, review thumbnail/title click-through and audience retention, especially the opening. Improve based on evidence; do not promise traction.
